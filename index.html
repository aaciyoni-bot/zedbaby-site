About ZedBaby

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the products genuine and new?

Yes. Every item we ship is brand new and sealed, sourced from top-rated international suppliers — the same factories that supply baby shops across Africa.

Do I need a credit card or bank account?

No! That's the whole point of ZedBaby. All you need is a Mobile Money wallet — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha — with enough balance for your order.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 12–25 days, depending on the item and your location in Zambia. We email you tracking updates along the way.

Are the products safe for my baby?

We focus on well-rated items from established sellers, and every listing shows the material, size and age range. Always check the age guidance on the listing, and message us on WhatsApp if you'd like help choosing the right item for your baby's age.

Is the price I see the final price?

The price shown includes our service and delivery handling. At checkout a small service fee (5%) is added to cover mobile money charges — the total you see at checkout is exactly what you pay.

What if my order arrives faulty or never comes?

You're covered by our money-back guarantee: if your order doesn't arrive, or arrives faulty, we refund you in full to your mobile money wallet. Just email us at office@orizisgroup.com with your order number.

How do I talk to a real person?

Tap the green Chat on WhatsApp button in our support section — our team replies 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00. Keep your order number handy so we can help faster.

Need help choosing?

Orders, tracking, refunds or just choosing the right size or item for your baby — email our team at office@orizisgroup.com, 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00.
